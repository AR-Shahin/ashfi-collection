Policies

Delivery, returns and privacy at Ashfi Collection.

These policies are sample text for the demo site. Please review and adapt them to your actual business terms before launch.

Delivery Policy

We deliver across all 64 districts of Bangladesh through trusted courier partners.

AreaEstimated timeCharge
Inside Dhaka1–2 working days
Outside Dhaka3–5 working days

  • Orders are confirmed by phone before dispatch.
  • Please keep your phone available on the delivery day.
  • Cash on Delivery is available nationwide.

Return & Exchange

  • Exchange requests must be made within 7 days of receiving the product.
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and in their original packaging with tags attached.
  • If you receive a damaged or wrong item, contact us within 24 hours with photos and we will arrange a replacement.
  • Delivery charges for change-of-mind exchanges are paid by the customer.

Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This demo site stores your cart, wishlist, coupon, customer account, saved addresses and orders only in your own browser (localStorage) and does not send personal information to any server. Your password is stored only as a salted hash. You can remove this data at any time by clearing your browser’s site data.

When the store is connected to a backend, we will only collect the details needed to deliver your order (name, phone and address) and will never sell your information to third parties.
