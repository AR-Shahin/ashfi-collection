Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?
Inside Dhaka usually 1–2 working days, and 3–5 working days for the rest of Bangladesh.
Do you offer Cash on Delivery?
Yes. You can pay in cash when your order arrives, anywhere in Bangladesh.
Can I exchange a product?
Yes – see our Return & Exchange policy for the full conditions.
How do I choose my size?
Each product page lists the available sizes. If you are between sizes or unsure, message us with your measurements and we will help you choose.
